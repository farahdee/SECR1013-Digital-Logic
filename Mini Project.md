## Reflection
This project gave me hands-on experience in applying digital logic theory to a real-world control system. I designed and built an electronic lift controller using a 3-bit synchronous counter with D flip-flops, which helped me better understand how sequential circuits work in practice.

Using the DEEDS Simulator allowed me to test my design and see how combinational and sequential circuits interact with each other. At first, I struggled with deriving the next-state logic and debugging the circuit, but through research and repeated testing in DEEDS, I was able to solve those issues. Overall, the project strengthened my analytical thinking, problem-solving ability, teamwork, and confidence in designing digital systems
